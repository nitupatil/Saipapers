# 16:9 Sai Paper Product promotional banners

Eleven lightweight **1600 × 900 SVG** banners are used in the homepage slideshow.

Each contains an embedded, already-compressed WebP marketing illustration with a navy/red title panel. No external image requests, web font assets or build step are required to display a banner. The slideshow loads its first and next slides first and loads further slides as needed.

The banner images are **AI-generated marketing concepts**. They are not guaranteed to match specific manufactured batches, supplied equipment models, label dimensions or certifications. The site product pages contain illustrative information and ask customers to confirm commercial specifications.

Slideshow: autoplay around every 6.2 seconds; manual arrows, pagination, swipe support, keyboard controls, pause button and prefers-reduced-motion support.
