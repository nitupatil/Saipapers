## Website integration notes

- Main product image: `direct-thermal-labels-studio-hero.webp`.
- Catalogue card image: `direct-thermal-labels-multiple-roll-sizes.webp`.
- Preserve full product visibility in desktop and mobile galleries using sized containers with `object-fit: contain`, and lazy load supporting images.
- Images are **AI-generated illustrations**, not verified photographs of inventory or endorsements by actual customers. Printed barcodes in usage images may not be scannable.
- Direct thermal labels use heat-sensitive paper and a compatible direct thermal printer **without thermal-transfer ribbon**. Confirm label dimensions, adhesive, roll core, environmental suitability, and stock availability before quoting.
