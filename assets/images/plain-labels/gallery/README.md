- These are **AI-generated illustrations**, not verified photos of actual inventory, customers or employees. Actual paper stock, adhesive, roll core, labels per roll, dimensions and compatible printers depend on the selected specification and should be confirmed with the sales team.
- Blank labels require suitable printing or handwritten information as appropriate. Do not claim preprinted data, waterproof properties or specific adhesive performance without confirmation.
