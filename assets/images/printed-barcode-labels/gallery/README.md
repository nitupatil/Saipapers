## Website integration

- Main gallery / homepage hero: `printed-barcode-labels-studio-hero.webp`.
- Shop listing / product card: `printed-barcode-labels-multiple-rolls.webp`.
- Preserve full image visibility using `object-fit: contain`, with bounded image heights for mobile and desktop.
- Use the individual alt text suggestions above; lazy-load below-the-fold images.
- **Disclosure:** All images are AI-generated product illustrations, not confirmed photographs of inventory, staff, customers, or real transactions. Barcodes and displayed numbers are conceptual, may not be valid/scannable, and must not be used as real EAN/UPC/GTIN identifiers.
- Confirm product material, adhesive, label dimensions, printing technology, roll core, price, availability, and barcode quality with the sales team.
