## Recommended website use

- Homepage product slider and product gallery hero: `shipping-labels-studio-hero.webp`.
- Shop catalogue/product listing: `shipping-labels-multiple-rolls-display.webp`.
- Show supporting images for packaging, dispatch, printing, product angles and business use cases; use `object-fit: contain` to avoid cropping on desktop and mobile.
- Use distinct alt text from the table above, lazy-load supporting images and preserve dimensions to minimize layout shift.
- Images are **AI-generated conceptual illustrations**, not actual photographs of stock, customers or staff; example addresses, tracking numbers and barcodes are fictional and are **not guaranteed to be valid or scannable**.
- Confirm actual available adhesive, label dimensions, printer compatibility and order quantity with the sales team. No specific courier compatibility is implied by these illustrations.
