Shipping Label Size Guide: 4×6, 100×150 mm and More

A practical guide to shipping-label formats, printer compatibility and selecting label stock for courier and e-commerce parcels.

Need help choosing?

Speak to Sai Paper Product before placing a bulk order. Printer compatibility, material and application conditions matter.

A shipping label should be readable, attach securely to the parcel, and match the layout used by the shipping service. Choosing the correct size matters for addresses, QR codes and tracking barcodes.

Common shipping label dimensions

Popular formats include 100 × 75 mm and around 100 × 150 mm. The 4 × 6 inch format measures approximately 101.6 × 152.4 mm, so it is close to, but not exactly identical to, 100 × 150 mm.

Check your printer first

Before selecting a roll, check the supported print width, media sensor, core size and whether the device uses direct thermal paper or ribbon-based transfer printing.

Adhesive and storage conditions

Courier cartons and plastic packaging can have different surface requirements. Share parcel materials and any moisture or temperature considerations when seeking a quote.
