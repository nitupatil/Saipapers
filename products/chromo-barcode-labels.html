BUYER QUESTIONS

Chromo barcode label FAQs

What are chromo barcode labels made from?

Chromo barcode labels are generally coated paper self-adhesive labels. The white roll pictured is a non-metallic paper example, not silver or gold foil. Confirm the available coating and adhesive before ordering.

Do chromo barcode labels require a printer ribbon?

Chromo paper barcode labels are typically printed using thermal-transfer technology with an appropriate ribbon. Tell us your printer model so the correct media and ribbon combination can be checked.

Can I order custom label sizes or roll cores?

You can enquire about label width, height, roll core, roll direction and quantity. Availability and exact specifications should be confirmed with the sales team before an order.

Are these labels suitable for retail shops and billing?

They can be used for retail product identification and POS barcode labeling when correctly printed and scanned. The barcode must be generated from valid inventory data and tested with your equipment.

Can chromo paper labels be used on bottles and plastic boxes?

Potentially, depending on the adhesive, surface texture and environment. Test a sample on your packaging, especially if it may encounter moisture, oil, temperature changes or abrasion.

What is the difference between chromo and direct thermal labels?

Chromo coated-paper labels generally use a thermal-transfer ribbon. Direct thermal labels have a heat-sensitive coating and normally print without a ribbon. Use the media intended for your printer and application.

What information is needed for a bulk quote?

Share label dimensions, quantity, core size, roll direction, finish preference, intended surface, printer model and whether the labels need blank or preprinted artwork.
