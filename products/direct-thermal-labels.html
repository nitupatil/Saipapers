Direct Thermal Labels

Direct thermal label rolls help print shipping, tracking and short-life identification labels without an ink ribbon. A practical option for busy dispatch counters and retail operations.

Direct thermal paper reacts to heat from the printhead, so a separate transfer ribbon is not needed. Depending on usage, choose standard or top-coated stock and confirm the appropriate adhesive, size and roll core.

  • No ink ribbon required
  • Standard or top-coated paper options
  • Custom size and adhesive enquiries
  • Used for shipping, warehousing and retail

BUYER QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Do direct thermal labels need ribbon?

No. Compatible direct thermal printers use heat-sensitive media without a ribbon.

Are these labels suitable for long outdoor use?

Direct thermal prints can darken or fade under heat, light and abrasion. Discuss the environment before selecting the material.

What information is required for a quote?

Share label width × height, quantity, core size and printer model.
