Ten illustrations show unprinted white adhesive label rolls at multiple angles and in retail, packing, warehouse, dispensing and packaging environments. Select the specification that suits your actual task.

01 / Product overview

Blank white self-adhesive labels

Unprinted adhesive label rolls can be used for custom details applied or printed later, such as item names, price stickers and internal stock IDs. Confirm paper stock, adhesive and core size before ordering.

02 / Roll details

Review the paper and roll format

Label width, height, die-cut gaps, roll direction and core diameter determine whether a roll suits your dispenser or printer. These dimensions need to match your chosen equipment.

03 / Roll selection

Choose the dimensions for your business

Small labels can suit pricing and item marking; larger labels can accommodate product information or cartons. Supply exact dimensions and the approximate number of labels per roll for a quote.

04 / Label construction

Check the surface, liner and adhesive

The white label face is separated from a release liner and attached with adhesive. Paper, synthetic media and adhesive grades can behave differently on cardboard, plastic, metal or curved containers.

05 / Layout options

Review roll formats at a glance

For consistent results, confirm whether your workflow uses single-across or multi-across labels, as well as gap spacing, orientation and any application-machine requirements.

06 / Packing workflows

Mark packages during packing

Blank labels may be used to write or print order references and internal identifiers. For shipping barcodes or courier documents, use the required approved artwork and printer/media combination.

07 / Stock control

Prepare stock and storage identifiers

White labels can be printed with bin IDs, item codes or quantities for warehouse organization. Confirm the print technology and scan the final code if barcode information is needed.

08 / Retail use

Useful for shop pricing and item marking

Plain stickers can support manually written prices, product numbers, and stock notes. Permanent or removable adhesives should be selected based on the packaging surface and desired removal.

09 / Pack surfaces

Adapt labels to different containers

Paper, plastic mailers, jars, pouches and textured cartons may require different materials or adhesives. Test a label on the actual pack, especially for moisture, oil, cold or frequent handling.

Manual dispensers and automated applicators can have specific requirements for core size, roll width, winding and liner construction. Match the exact configuration before ordering.

BUYER QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions about plain labels

What is the difference between plain and preprinted labels?

Plain labels are supplied without printed data or artwork so a business can write, mark or print information itself. Preprinted labels already carry an approved design or data before use.

Can plain label rolls be used with a thermal printer?

Only if the label stock is appropriate for the printer. Direct thermal media prints with heat without a ribbon, while thermal-transfer media requires a suitable ribbon and printer. Confirm your printer model and label specification.

What label dimensions can I enquire about?

You can share width × height in millimetres, gap between labels, number across, labels per roll, winding direction and the roll core diameter. Actual availability depends on your requirements and quotation.

Do plain white labels have permanent or removable adhesive?

Adhesive options depend on the selected label construction and stock availability. Tell the sales team whether labels should stay fixed or be removable from a particular surface, and test on the actual product.

Can I apply these labels on cartons, plastic pouches and glass jars?

Potentially, if the adhesive, label material and surface conditions are compatible. Test samples on the packaging, particularly if it is wet, oily, cold, curved or textured.

Are all plain adhesive labels waterproof?

No. Plain paper labels are not automatically waterproof. Water resistance depends on face material, coating, adhesive, printing and exposure conditions; request and test appropriate materials when needed.

Can I print barcodes and pricing details onto blank labels?

Yes, with appropriately matched print media and equipment. Use valid barcode data, correctly formatted print artwork and a scanning test. Blank label rolls themselves do not contain printed barcodes.

What should I share for a bulk price quotation?

Provide label width and height, material and adhesive requirements, quantity, number of labels per roll, printer model or applicator type, core size, intended surface and delivery location.
