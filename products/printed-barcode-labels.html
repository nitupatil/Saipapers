BUYER QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions about printed barcode labels

Can different barcode numbers be printed in sequence?

Variable and sequential barcode printing can be discussed when you supply the required numbering scheme, format, quantity and data file. Final availability and proof approval must be confirmed before production.

What barcode formats can be used on printed labels?

Common formats include Code 128 and Code 39 for internal identification, EAN/UPC for retail product identifiers and QR or Data Matrix for applicable 2D uses. Share the target system and code specification for compatibility review.

Does a printed barcode automatically scan correctly?

No. Scanning depends on correct data, symbology, bar dimensions, contrast, quiet zones and print quality. Validate a real production sample with the intended scanner. Barcodes shown in gallery illustrations are not verified.

Can I use EAN or UPC numbers without registration?

Retail EAN/UPC barcodes generally require appropriately assigned GTINs through the relevant identifier system, such as GS1. The label printer reproduces your supplied number; printing a barcode does not assign an official product identifier.

Can these barcode labels go on cartons, jars or plastic packaging?

Potentially, depending on the adhesive, coating and application surface. Test samples on the actual packaging, especially when exposed to moisture, oil, heat or rough handling.

What label size, roll core or printing material should I choose?

Share the label width and height, application surface, required material, total quantity, roll core, winding direction and applicator or printer model. The available combination can then be reviewed for your workflow.

Can I order both black-and-white preprinted codes and branded artwork?

You can enquire about preprinted numeric, barcode and product-information layouts, including branding. Supply final artwork, encoded data, color requirements and quantity for a feasibility and quotation review.

What should I include in a bulk quotation request?

Provide artwork or barcode data, symbology, dimensions, material and adhesive needs, number of unique codes, total labels required, core specification and expected application conditions.
