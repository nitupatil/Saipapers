BUYER QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions about printed product labels

Can I print my own logo and packaging design?

You can submit your approved logo, print-ready artwork, label measurements and quantities for a custom printing enquiry. Available printing options and an artwork proof should be confirmed before production.

Which label material should I choose: chromo paper, PP or PET?

The best option depends on the package, moisture or oil exposure, product handling and budget. Paper stock may suit many dry-pack applications, while suitable film stocks may be discussed for more demanding conditions. Samples should be tested on the real surface.

Can printed labels be matte, glossy or laminated?

Finish and lamination options depend on the printing process and material. Ask the sales team to confirm the desired finish, availability and any effect on flexibility or product application.

Are printed product labels suitable for food jars and cosmetic packaging?

Labels can be considered for these packages when the adhesive, material, ink and application method are suitable. The customer must review all applicable packaging, ingredient, safety and statutory labeling requirements before printing.

Can I order different designs for multiple products?

You can enquire about multiple SKUs or variants by sharing each label design, the number of unique artworks, measurements and quantity per artwork. Cost and minimum order quantities are confirmed through quotation.

What artwork file and dimensions should I provide?

Provide a print-ready file where possible, plus exact width and height, shape, cut line, bleed if required, important text and desired colors. Final production-file specifications should be confirmed with the printer.

Will printed product labels be waterproof or oil-resistant?

Not automatically. Performance depends on the substrate, coating, lamination, ink and adhesive. Explain expected exposure to water, oil, cold or rubbing and test a suitable physical sample.

What is required to request a bulk quotation?

Share label dimensions, material, finish, artwork, number of designs, quantity for each design, container surface, roll or sheet format, winding or core requirements, and delivery location.
