BUYER QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions about shipping labels

Are 4 × 6 inch and 100 × 150 mm shipping labels identical?

No. Four by six inches is 101.6 × 152.4 mm. Although both formats are used for parcel labeling, dimensions differ slightly. Confirm the exact label setting in your printer and carrier or marketplace template.

Can these labels be printed without a ribbon?

Direct thermal labels print with a compatible direct thermal printer without an ink ribbon. Thermal-transfer media requires a compatible ribbon and thermal-transfer printer. Tell us your printer type before selecting a roll.

Which printer details are needed to choose a shipping label roll?

Provide the printer brand and model, the required label width and height, roll core diameter, gap or mark sensor requirements, media type and approximate roll diameter. These affect compatibility.

Can these labels be used for different courier or e-commerce services?

Potentially, provided the label dimensions, material and printer match that service's current template and shipping process. No courier compatibility is guaranteed by the marketing illustrations.

Can I order blank or preprinted shipping labels?

Share whether you need blank label stock to print variable address and tracking details yourself or a preprinted layout. Availability, custom artwork and order quantities should be confirmed by the sales team.

What adhesive is suitable for boxes and mailers?

The best choice depends on whether the label goes onto corrugated cardboard, paper envelopes, plastic mailers or another surface, plus moisture and handling conditions. A physical test is recommended.

Are the barcodes and tracking numbers shown in these images real?

No. The gallery uses AI-generated illustrative shipping details, addresses and barcodes. They are not verified carrier records or guaranteed scannable codes. Generate operational labels through your shipping software.

How can I request a bulk shipping label quotation?

Share required width and height, quantity, material, adhesive preference, printer model, roll core size and intended use. The sales team can then confirm available configurations and pricing.
