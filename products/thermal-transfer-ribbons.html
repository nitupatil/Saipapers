Thermal Transfer Ribbons

Thermal-transfer ribbons help compatible printers create durable print on suitable label materials. Share the printer model and ribbon specifications for a precise enquiry.

Ribbon compatibility depends on its width, length, ink side, core and ink formulation. A correctly matched ribbon can help achieve legible results on chromo, film or other thermal-transfer media.

  • Printer compatibility assistance
  • Wax, wax-resin or resin requirements on enquiry
